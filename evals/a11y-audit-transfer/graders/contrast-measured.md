PASS if the audit reports that text contrast fails WCAG 1.4.3 for at least one of the balance line, the fee line or the recent amounts, AND gives a measured contrast ratio (a number such as 2.6:1) for at least one failing pair. FAIL if contrast failures are missing, or are claimed without any ratio.
