The user asked for a money transfer screen and gave no context about users, goals, constraints or success measures.

PASS if, before presenting any screen design, the assistant asks the user at least one question about who the users are, what they are trying to get done, the constraints (platform, design system, regulations), or how success will be measured. A question asked through a question tool counts.

FAIL if the assistant presents a finished or near-finished screen (a layout, a component list for the screen, a wireframe, code, or a detailed spec) without first asking any such question, or if it states assumptions and proceeds to design without asking.
