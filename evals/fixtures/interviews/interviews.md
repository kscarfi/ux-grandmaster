# Interview notes: freelancers using Splitly (5 sessions, November 2026)

## P1, designer, 34
- Splits rent and groceries with two flatmates.
- "I never know if someone already paid me back, I check my bank app instead."
- Stopped adding small expenses: "too many taps for a coffee."

## P2, developer, 29
- Uses it for trips only.
- Liked the summary at the end of a trip.
- "Adding an expense takes too long when everyone is waiting to pay."

## P3, photographer, 41
- Splits studio costs with a partner.
- "I need to attach the receipt for my accountant." Uses a separate folder now.
- Checks who owes whom every week.

## P4, translator, 26
- Shares a flat with three people.
- "I don't trust the balance. Last month it was wrong because someone edited an old expense."
- Would like to see the history of changes.

## P5, consultant, 38
- Uses it for team dinners with clients.
- "Typing amounts in different currencies is a pain."
- Settles up rarely; forgets who paid back.
