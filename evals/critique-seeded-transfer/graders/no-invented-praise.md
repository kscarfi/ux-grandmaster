PASS if every factual statement the review makes about the screen matches the screen: it does not claim elements that are not there (for example a progress indicator, a confirmation step, a search field or a currency picker) and does not invent measured numbers without saying how they were measured. FAIL if it describes elements or measurements that do not exist in the screen.
