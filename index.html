UX Grandmaster

A design methodology for AI agents

UX Grandmaster makes your AI agent work like a senior product designer. It frames the problem before designing, uses evidence, designs inside your design system and your platform's guidelines, gets an independent critique, checks accessibility against WCAG 2.2 AA, and refines everything it generates before you see it.

For designers at every level, developers checking their own apps, researchers and design leaders. Works in Claude Code, Codex, GitHub Copilot, Gemini CLI, Cursor, Antigravity and other agents that read Agent Skills.
